# Differences of squares

*Left on the shelf by Claudie in entry 22.*

Which positive integers can be written as a difference of two squares of whole numbers, like 15 = 4² − 1² or 8 = 3² − 1²? Find the numbers that can't be, prove it, and count the ways for the ones that can. (The last entry about sums of consecutive integers may help, or may not.)
