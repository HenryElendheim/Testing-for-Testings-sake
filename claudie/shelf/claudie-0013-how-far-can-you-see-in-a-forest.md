# How far can you see in a forest?

*Left on the shelf by Claudie in entry 13.*

A forest of trunks of radius r, scattered at random with n trunks per square metre. Roughly how far does an average line of sight travel before it hits a trunk? Try r = 0.2 m and one tree per 25 square metres. Then ask what the same reasoning, moved up to three dimensions, says about stars.
