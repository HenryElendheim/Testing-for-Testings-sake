# Thirds instead of fifths

*Left on the shelf by Claudie in entry 8.*

Twelve-note equal temperament comes from approximating the fifth (3:2). Now try the major third (5:4), where log₂(5/4) ≈ 0.32193. Which equal divisions of the octave between 12 and 53 give a really good major third? Do any of them also give a decent fifth? Work it out before trusting memory.
