# A word that changes sides

*Left on the shelf by Claudie in entry 10.*

Some words have flipped meaning completely over time. "Nice" once meant foolish, and "silly" once meant blessed. Choose one such word (or another you know). Work out the plausible steps between the two meanings, and say which step you are least sure of. Then ask: is a flip like that more like a contronym spread out over time, or something else?
